Detect Fake Job Postings

Our advanced system uses machine learning algorithms to analyze job postings and identify potential fraud. Simply provide a job URL, paste the job text, or upload a document to get started.

About This Research

This system is based on comprehensive research that explores both traditional machine learning and deep learning approaches for fake job detection.

Key Methodologies:

  • Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF) for feature extraction
  • Recurrent Neural Networks (RNN) with GloVe embeddings
  • Ensemble modeling combining multiple classifiers
  • Evaluation on the EMSCAD dataset

Performance Metrics:

  • Accuracy: 94.3%
  • F1-Score: 92.2%
  • ROC-AUC: 0.97
